# RISK_REGISTER.md — CardDemo COBOL-to-Java Migration Risk Register

Companion to `MODERNIZATION_BLUEPRINT.md`, `DOMAIN_DECOMPOSITION.md` and `CUTOVER_PLAN.md`. Bounded-context names, program lists and file names are the shared ground-truth used by all four documents (e.g. **Transactions**, **Interest/Statements/Reporting**, **Account Management**, files `ACCTDATA`, `CARDXREF`, `TCATBALF`, `TRANSACT`).

Scoring: Likelihood and Impact are High/Med/Low. Owner is the bounded context (or the migration workstream) that carries the mitigation. Line numbers refer to the current `feature/praveen-java-migration-base` source and were verified by inspection (see final section).

## Cross-cutting notes

- Risks R1, R5 and R7 all concentrate in `CBACT04C`; it should be the first program under characterization testing and the last to be cut over.
- R4 and R6 share a root cause (multi-file writes to `ACCTDATA`) — resolving the Account Management seam in `DOMAIN_DECOMPOSITION.md` closes both.
- R2 and R10 are platform/cutover risks owned outside any single bounded context; their status should be reviewed at every wave gate in `CUTOVER_PLAN.md`.

Discrepancies versus the shared ground-truth (ground-truth names retained above):

- The optional modules live under `app/` (`app/app-authorization-ims-db2-mq/`, `app/app-transaction-type-db2/`, `app/app-vsam-mq/`), not at the repository root.
- `app/cbl/` contains 31 source files, not 30: the extra ones are `COBSWAIT.cbl` (batch wait utility) and `CSUTLDTC.cbl` (date utility program), neither in the ground-truth context lists. `CBSTM03A`/`CBSTM03B` use the uppercase `.CBL` extension.
- Interest formula is at L464-465 (ground-truth ~462, which is the `1300-COMPUTE-INTEREST` paragraph header); DEFAULT fallback is at L436-439 (~436 matches).
- `CBTRN02C` SELECTs `TRANSACT-FILE ASSIGN TO TRANFILE` (DD `TRANFILE`, dataset `TRANSACT.VSAM.KSDS`); `CBTRN03C` assigns the xref to DD `CARDXREF` whereas `CBTRN02C`/`CBACT04C`/`CBSTM03B` use `XREFFILE` — same dataset, different DD names.
- Account money fields in `CVACT01Y` are `PIC S9(10)V99`; the `S9(9)V99` in the ground-truth applies to `TRAN-CAT-BAL` (`CVTRA01Y`) and `TRAN-AMT` (`CVTRA05Y`).
- `COPY CSUTLDPY` is found only in `COACTUPC` within `app/cbl/`; "widely reused" holds for the date-utility family (`CSUTLDWY`, `CSDAT01Y`, `CSUTLDTC`) rather than for `CSUTLDPY` alone.
